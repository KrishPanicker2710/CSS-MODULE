The Earthquake

On 26 December 2004, a massive undersea earthquake occurred off the western coast of northern Sumatra, Indonesia. The earthquake measured approximately 9.1 magnitude and caused the seafloor to move suddenly. This displaced a huge amount of seawater and generated a powerful tsunami.

The Science Behind It

The earthquake occurred at a major subduction zone. The India Plate was moving beneath the Burma microplate. Stress built up until the fault suddenly ruptured.

Impact on India

India was one of the countries affected by the 2004 Indian Ocean tsunami. Coastal regions of Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Kerala and the Andaman and Nicobar Islands were affected.

Lessons Learned

🌊 Tsunami Warning Systems

The disaster highlighted the importance of early warning systems in tsunami-prone regions.

🚨 Emergency Preparedness

Communities need evacuation plans and disaster-preparedness education.

📡 Communication

Fast communication can help people receive warnings and reach safer areas.
