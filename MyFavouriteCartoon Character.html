My Favourite Cartoon Character

My favourite cartoon character is the lion Kion from, the Lion Gaurd. This is a continuation of the Lion King movie. In this series, Simba has two children, Kiara and Kion. Kiara, being the oldest, is the future queen of the Pride Lands.

Kion discovers that he has the gift of the roar of the elders. This gift makes him the leader of the Lion Gaurd, a group of lion of the Pride Land's fastest, bravest, strongest, fiercest and keenest of sight. He chooses a hippopotamus, Bestie, as the strongest. A honeybadger, Bunga, as the bravest. A cheetah, Fuli, as the fastest and an egret, Ono, as the keenest of sight.

Here is a picture of the lion guard.

Kion starts leading the Lion Guard against the other animals who want to disturb the 'Circle of Life'. In difficult times, he is advised by the spirit of his grandfather, Mufasa. However, the other animals find a way to raise the spirit of Scar, Mufasa's step brother, who was one of the leaders of the Lion Guard. Scar used his roar for evil purposes and ends up losing the roar completely.

He helped the Janja and his hyenas unit the outlands agianst the Pride Lands. But following Mufasa's advice Kion always managed to defend the Pride Lands.

Here is a picture of Scar.

Lion Guard with the help of the other animals of the Pride Lands stormed the outlands and destroyed Scar and his allies. However, during this attack Kion sustains an injury from a snake bite which starts to poison his mind.

This is how Kion looked after the injury.

The Lion Guard rushes to find the Tree of Life which is the only thing that can cure Kion from turning evil. Kion is cured from the venomous bite after being treated by the Night Pride. The Night Pride is a group of lions who protect the Tree of Life. Kion is informed that Zira and her troop of evil lions are invading the Pride Lands to make Kovu the king of the Pride Lands. Kion foils Zira's plans but spares Kovu after he sees that Kovu had saved Kiara's life.
